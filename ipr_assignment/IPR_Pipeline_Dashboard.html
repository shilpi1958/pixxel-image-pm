How to read this dashboard
Image / attempt
Image = one ordered capture. Attempt = one processing run. Image metrics use processable images; step metrics use attempts.
Processable
Captured and under 70% cloud. Others are excluded from delivery metrics.
Delivered
Passed QC and uploaded. Uploaded without a QC pass does not count.
On time
Delivered by the SLA deadline: acquisition + attempt-1 SLA hours.
Late
Not delivered and past the SLA deadline.
Expected ETA
Estimated from remaining steps and past attempts at the same priority. None is shown for stuck or not-captured images.
Partial coverage
Delivered, but the export marks Target Status = Partial, so part of the customer's area is missing. Tell the customer before they notice.
Internal accounts
Calibration and Internal Demo. They count in pipeline views, not customer views.
